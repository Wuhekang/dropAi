# Mechanical Reasoning

Use this skill when a mechanical product, mechanism, CAD package, engineering drawing, or design review must be generated.

## Required Flow

1. Analyze requirements before proposing structure.
2. Compare candidate mechanisms before choosing one.
3. Build a force path and identify critical parts.
4. Select materials based on strength, weight, cost, environment, and manufacturability.
5. Check manufacturing, assembly, maintenance, and safety factor.
6. Produce a design review report.

## Output Requirements

The answer or generated artifact must include:
- Requirement analysis
- Mechanism candidates
- Recommended mechanism and reasons
- ForceReport
- MaterialDecision
- ManufacturingPlan
- DesignReviewReport

## Prohibited

Do not directly generate the final structure without first showing the reasoning chain.
